# Record-Box Playlists

**Mode:** Formal

*(Part of the loop-rethink sequence — see the design for ordering and context.)*

## Intent

A playlist becomes the box of records planned for the set: an easy selector loading any of its tracks onto any deck. Sequential auto-play through a deck — today's attach-and-advance — becomes an opt-in option (its home decided here), available in Playback and Beat modes; a clip-looping deck never auto-advances. End-of-track warning suppressed in Clip mode.

Design: [loop-rethink](../archive/2026-08-15-loop-rethink.md).

2026-08-22: make the two uses explicit as **two playlist modes** — a **picker** mode, the record box, loading any of its tracks onto any deck; and an **auto-play** mode, playing through one after the other on a deck. Today's attach-and-advance is the second; the first is the one the design calls primary.
